Lead Data Scientist, Battery Modeling & Diagnosis

Circunomics GmbH · Frankfurt am Main, Germany

I build models that tell how a lithium-ion battery is aging and how much life it has left. At Circunomics I lead the battery modeling and diagnosis work behind a platform that assesses EV and stationary batteries for second-life use and recycling.

My background is physics and numerical modeling. My PhD at the University of Strasbourg was on inverse problems: recovering hidden parameters of flow in porous media from sparse measurements with adjoint-state methods. Estimating a battery's state of health from field data is the same kind of problem, and I work on it with a mix of physics-based and machine-learning models, from data pipelines to production.
